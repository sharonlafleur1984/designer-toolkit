# Designer toolkit voice

**Last updated:** October 19, 2026

This is how the toolkit sounds in its own screens, the wiki, code comments and pull requests. The craft rules (plain language, no flattery, no words that can hurt) come from the [ux-writer skill](https://github.com/sharonlafleur1984/how-i-work/blob/main/skills/ux-writer/SKILL.md). This file sets the voice for each audience. A prototype's own copy belongs to that prototype, never to the toolkit.

**In one line:** a calm studio assistant who has already looked, says what it found, and lets you decide.

**What this voice is not:** a dashboard that shouts, a wizard that cheers you along, or a voice that talks over the prototype.

## Who we're talking to

**Sharon, working in the toolkit**
- Where: the shell's screens, like Add a prototype, Here's what I found and Scenarios
- Fun: low
- Sounds like: a colleague who read every file first. "Here's what I found," then a recommendation she can keep, edit or ignore.

**Stakeholders and developers opening a shared link**
- Where: the toolbar, the details page, notes
- Fun: off
- Sounds like: a clear label on every control, so nobody has to ask what a switch does

**People reading the docs**
- Where: the wiki, the README, code comments
- Fun: off
- Sounds like: a teammate leaving a useful note

**Sharon, reviewing a pull request**
- Where: pull request descriptions ("Your turn" first)
- Fun: off
- Sounds like: [working-with-sharon](https://github.com/sharonlafleur1984/how-i-work/blob/main/skills/working-with-sharon/SKILL.md): the one thing to look at, the one thing to decide, then what's next

## Turn the fun down for

Everything a stakeholder or developer sees. The prototype is the thing worth looking at.

## Emoji

None.

## Words we use

Pick one word for a thing and never switch. The full list is in [Spec, What the words mean](wiki/Spec.md#what-the-words-mean).

- **prototype**, not project or app: a product build the toolkit frames
- **shell**, not chrome or wrapper: the sidebar, toolbar and panels around a prototype
- **page state**, not screen state or variant: one named view of a page, with its own link
- **version**, not release or build: one Netlify deploy
- **persona**, not user type: who the prototype is shown as
- **scenario**: a persona with its context and login state, together
- **Keep, Edit, Ignore**: what you can do with each thing the toolkit found
- **theme**, not skin, in new writing: the toolkit wears Lantern's Fireside theme

## Sample lines

These come from the toolkit's own screens.

- **A finding:** "Here's what I found," with a count of what's being added above "Add to the toolkit" and "Not now"
- **Empty:** "Add your first milestone, or bring them in from GitHub." with the buttons "Add milestone" and "Import from GitHub"
- **An error, with its fix:** "Couldn't reach [site]. Check the link and your connection, then try again."
- **Privacy:** "Private to you and your team."
